# Brand Context

_Draft, 30 Oct 2026._

- **Name:** Morva, from "morph": shapes that keep changing. Formerly "Poster Generator". Domains to check: morva.studio, morva.art.

## Basics
- **What it is:** a browser-based visualiser and reference library of generative visuals built with p5.js, three.js and GLSL. You can play with each one live, see how it works, export it, and copy its code.
- **Category:** generative design tool, effect library and, eventually, an archive of techniques.
- **Stage:** early. v0.2 has 15 visuals, an Explore universe, type layouts and exports.
- **Website:** https://faheemmuhamed.github.io/poster-generator/

## Audience
- **Primary:** graphic and visual designers. They want striking generative, image and type effects without writing code, and want to understand what is possible.
- **Secondary:** creative developers. They want a working reference and clean code to start from.
- **Problem it solves:** generative techniques are scattered across Shadertoy, OpenProcessing, tutorials and GitHub. They come as code-first, unexplained and hard to tweak or export. Designers can't easily use them; developers can't easily browse them.

## Content scope
Content will grow over time:
- **Generative visuals:** noise, particles, simulation, 3D.
- **Image-based effects:** dithering, halftone, pixel sorting, displacement, glitch.
- **Kinetic type.**
- More to come.

## Positioning
- **Different because:** every entry is tweakable live, explained in plain language, exportable (PNG, video, embed) and comes with its code. It's a library, not a feed.
- **Alternatives:** Shadertoy, OpenProcessing, the p5.js editor, the three.js examples, Unicorn Studio, Spline, Cables.gl and Hydra.
- **Market position:** free, open, niche-craft, and supported by donations.

## Personality (draft)
- Curious, precise, generous, crafted, quietly playful.
- Tone: casual and clear. It's expert without being technical, and talks like a good studio colleague, not a developer manual.

## Values and mission
- **Values:** openness (code shown, credit given), craft, curiosity, access (no code needed).
- **Mission (working):** make the craft of generative visuals visible, usable and kept, for every designer.

## Goals (12 months)
- Grow the library across generative, image and type effects.
- Become a known reference among designers.
- Build donations and feedback through Gumroad and Tally.
- Long term: an archival library of techniques with history and credits.
